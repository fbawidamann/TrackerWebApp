Exercises prototype

A clickable version of docs/design/screens/exercises.md. It includes the Exercises tab, the picker from the workout, the detail page with its chart, and custom exercises. The example data is a year of training up to Tuesday, 29 September.

Mode

App theme

Things to try

  • Search: try inc db, pullup or curl. Search for something that doesn't exist to see "Create".
  • Open the filter button: sort, muscle, equipment and "show hidden". Remove active filters with the chips.
  • Open Bench Press (Barbell): drag across the chart, switch Weight/Reps and 3M/6M/1Y/All.
  • Open Pull-up, a bodyweight exercise, which only has a Reps chart.
  • History and About tabs. On About, try "Show all" under the instructions.
  • Use ⋯ to hide an exercise, then find it again with "Show hidden".
  • Use + to create your own exercise (try the name "Bench Press" with Barbell for the warning), then edit or delete it.
  • Picker mode: tick several exercises, then "Add".

Not in this prototype

Real catalog images (shown as placeholders), the desktop master–detail layout, Replace mode, and paging the History tab.
